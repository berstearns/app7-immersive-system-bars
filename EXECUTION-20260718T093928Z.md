`server-config.yaml` fallback. Files changed in this folder:

- `.env`: `BACKEND_URL` + `QUEUE_HOST` → `129.212.166.125`
- `androidApp/server-config.yaml`, `desktopApp/server-config.yaml`,
  `desktopApp/src/main/resources/server-config.yaml`: `backend_url`/`queue_host`
- `desktopApp/.../desktop/Main.kt`: `DEFAULT_BACKEND_URL`
